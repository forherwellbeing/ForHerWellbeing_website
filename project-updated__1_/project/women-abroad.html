For women living abroad

Root-cause care, wherever you’ve landed.

Moving countries doesn’t mean starting over on your health. Every program runs fully online, timed around you.

Built for
the diaspora

Many of Dr. Raga’s clients are Indian women living in Germany, the US, the UK and the Gulf, navigating unfamiliar healthcare systems, new food environments and the stress of settling into a new country, often while symptoms they had at home get worse rather than better.

Having lived and worked in Germany herself for 11 years, Dr. Raga understands this specific experience directly: the isolation of not having your usual support system, and the challenge of applying root-cause nutrition advice inside a foreign grocery store.

ForHerWellbeing’s programs are delivered entirely online, so you get the same rigorous investigation and personalized plan, adapted to what’s actually available where you live.

Common questions from clients abroad

Do I need to get labs done in India?

No. You can use any local lab in your country of residence; Dr. Raga will tell you exactly which panels to request and interpret the results once you share them.

Will the diet plan work with food available where I live?

Yes. Nutrition guidance is adapted to what's realistically available in your local grocery stores, not built around Indian-only ingredients.

How do payments work for international clients?

Payments can be made via international card or bank transfer; details are shared after your Discovery Call.

What if the timezone doesn't work for a live call?

Reach out on WhatsApp with your location and availability; slots are scheduled individually to find a time that works for both sides.
