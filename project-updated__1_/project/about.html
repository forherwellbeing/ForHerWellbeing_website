About Dr. Raga

A scientist who has also lived the struggle.

Not a distant expert. Not a lifestyle influencer. A researcher who built her method the same way she once had to find her own answers.

Dr. Raga Deepthi
From the lab
to the clinic

Dr. Raga Deepthi holds a PhD from Heidelberg University and carries an IIT Bombay association, built over 11 years of professional experience in Germany working at the intersection of biochemistry and human health.

Her approach to women’s wellness didn’t start in a classroom. It started with her own experience navigating an autoimmune condition: the confusion of symptoms that didn’t add up, the frustration of generic advice that didn’t work, and the eventual relief of finally understanding what her own body was telling her.

That experience is why ForHerWellbeing exists. To give other women the same rigorous, root-cause investigation she once had to find for herself, grounded in science and delivered with the understanding of someone who has actually been there.
