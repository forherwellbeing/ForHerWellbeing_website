What happens on the Discovery Call?

Dr. Raga will ask about your symptoms, history and any labs you already have, then recommend whether Yoga, Diet Coaching or Gut Metabolism Reset is the right starting point for you.

Yes. All programs and the Discovery Call are available over video call to clients abroad; see the Women Abroad page for details specific to international clients.

Is the ₹1,000 call fee adjusted later?

Yes, if you enrol in a paid program afterward, the Discovery Call fee is credited against your program cost.

What if I'm not sure which program I need?

That's exactly what the Discovery Call is for. Select "Not sure yet" in the inquiry form, or mention it on WhatsApp, and Dr. Raga will help you decide.
